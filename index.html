02

Research

PhD research · Work in progress

Rethinking how we see gigapixel 360° worlds.

My current research investigates how projection geometry, viewport selection, multiresolution sampling, and delivery can be optimized together for responsive, high-fidelity exploration of massive spherical images.

360°
QoE LoD GPU Tiles
Active research DAPVS / 2026

Distortion-Aware Projection & Viewport Selection

A continuity-aware framework for interactive gigapixel 360° imagery. The work asks whether representation, orientation, projection, level of detail, and tile delivery should be selected as one coordinated decision instead of isolated steps.

Representation → Orientation → Viewport → Delivery
  • Omnidirectional imaging
  • Projection geometry
  • Quality of experience
  • Progressive delivery
  • WebGL rendering
